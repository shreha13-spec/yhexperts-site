You've likely heard from someone on our team at @yhexpertsglobal.com. YH Experts Global is a real, working extension of Yuwahub — the student and youth community platform at yuwahub.com, active since its founding in Nepal. We're not a cold-outreach shell. We're the team that turns Yuwahub's community reach into structured work with experts, and this site exists so you can verify exactly who you're talking to before you reply.

What we actually do

In plain terms, before anything else.

We find people who are genuinely good at what they do, sit down with them properly, and help them turn that expertise into something the Yuwahub community can learn from directly.

Yuwahub was built as a community — a place for young people in Nepal to find opportunities, join hubs, and meet people working toward the same things. It grew fast because the demand was real: students and early professionals wanted more than a feed, they wanted access.

YH Experts Global is how we deliver on that. We reach out to accomplished professionals across industries, have real conversations with them about how they actually work, and then build that knowledge into finished products — courses, workbooks, ebooks, media shows and training modules — credited to the expert and produced to a standard worth putting a name on.

How we move with an expert

The same sequence, every time — from first contact to a finished product.

01

Identify

We look across industries for people with real command over their craft — measured by their work, not their follower count.

02

Vet

We check their track record, references and body of work, and gauge whether they can actually explain what they know, not just do it.

03

Converse

We sit down for long-form, recorded conversations — the thinking behind the work, not a highlight reel or a polished bio.

04

Shape

Raw conversation and material get structured into a format that fits the audience — a course outline, a workbook, a show format.

05

Produce

Our in-house production house handles filming, editing, design and layout, so the finished product meets a publishing standard.

06

Release

We deliver it to the Yuwahub community and partner institutions, with the expert credited and involved through to launch.

Who we choose to work with

Not everyone we approach makes it through. These are the standards we hold.

A real body of work

Demonstrated results in their field — projects, clients or outcomes we can actually point to, not just a title.

The ability to teach

Doing something well and explaining it well are different skills. We look for both.

Depth over breadth

We'd rather work with someone with deep command of one thing than a generalist take on many.

Willingness to be candid

The material only works if it includes the real process — including what didn't work.

Relevance to our audience

Their expertise has to translate into something Nepal's youth and early professionals can genuinely use.

Staying power

We look for experts willing to stay involved past a single session — through review, updates and community Q&A.

Client confidentiality

Every expert and partner we work with shares material with us before it's public — draft curricula, unreleased footage, commercial terms, personal background. We treat all of it as confidential by default.

That means no unpublished work, names or terms are shared outside the immediate project team without explicit sign-off, and we're glad to formalise this with an NDA before any detailed conversation, if that's what a partnership needs.
